## Main Plot
- Current summary: Hyuk Kang is a former top-tier talent manager who left HS Entertainment to start his own agency. He formed Blue Sherbet — a girl group with an extraordinary lineup — but the venture failed, largely due to HS Entertainment's interference and his own passivity. Years later he is washed up. His former artist Hee-yeon Lee tracks him down, tries to convince him to start over, and fails. He passes out under a strangely glowing full moon and wakes up back in the past — not at the beginning of his independence, but after Blue Sherbet's first failed debut. Armed with the knowledge of everything that went wrong, he begins again.
- Key turning points: Resignation and confrontation with Director Park (Ch. 1 — past flashback). Hee-yeon Lee's visit and refusal to give up (Ch. 1 — future). The regression trigger (Ch. 1 — end). Waking up in his Ilsan office, confirmed regression (Ch. 2). Jin-ah's vocal assessment and Hyuk's crystallized motivation (Ch. 2 — end).
- Current position: End of Chapter 12. Hee-yeon's solo debut underway. "Wish Upon A Moon" peaked at #12. Director Park's first counter-move fully backfired. Golden Tree publicly allied. International fan influx anticipated.
- Unresolved threads: Why the regression lands at this specific point. The full scope of HS Entertainment's sabotage. Jung-han's fandom knowledge gap. LOAN's path to independence. Hae-yeol / Hee-yeon collaboration timing. Event fees rising.

## Subplots

### Hee-yeon Lee's Return
- Summary: Hee-yeon succeeded solo but feels hollow without Hyuk and her group. She sought him out in the future to repay a debt and restart. Her conviction that he was never wrong becomes Hyuk's driving motivation in the regression.
- Characters involved: Hee-yeon Lee, Hyuk Kang
- Status: Future timeline — resolved in Ch. 1. Ongoing in regression timeline.

### The HS Fallout
- Summary: HS Entertainment is the primary cause of Blue Sherbet's failure. Hyuk also blames himself — too passive, too careful. He knows now he needed to fight harder.
- Characters involved: Hyuk Kang, Director Park, HS Entertainment
- Status: Ongoing. Ch. 12: Director Park's planted articles backfired completely, accelerating Blue Sherbet's momentum instead of crushing it.

### Blue Sherbet's Potential
- Summary: Each member has extraordinary individual potential Hyuk failed to develop in the original timeline. The Avengers Plan corrects that — solo lanes first, group later.
- Characters involved: All four members + Hyuk
- Status: Ongoing. Hee-yeon solo debut underway. Jin-ah solo in planning. Yoon-jung streaming growing. Yumi composing accelerating.

## Themes & Motifs

### Talent vs. Power
- First appearance: Chapter 1
- Description: Hyuk's instincts are near-perfect, but talent-spotting alone couldn't protect him from power structures. Each regression move is an attempt to fight smarter, not just better.
- Recurring: Director Park's proverbs (Ch. 1), Hyuk's self-analysis (Ch. 2), every HS interference moment.

### The Cost of Integrity
- First appearance: Chapter 1
- Description: Hyuk's failure came partly from doing things the right way — no poaching, no conflict, too much trust. Passivity was the mistake, not integrity.
- Key line: "I shouldn't have read the room. I should have gone in like I wanted to kill them." (Ch. 2)

### Regret and the Longing to Go Back
- First appearance: Chapter 1
- Description: The regression literalizes the wish. But it doesn't give him a clean reset — he lands after the first failure, not before it.

### The Full Moon
- First appearance: Chapter 1
- Status: Confirmed as regression trigger (Ch. 2). Mechanism and meaning still open.

### Lions and Hyenas
- First appearance: Chapter 1
- Status: Not revisited through Ch. 12. May be Ch. 1-only framing. Flag if it recurs.

### Treating People Like People
- First appearance: Chapter 2
- Description: The entertainment industry treats artists as products. Hyuk can't do that — and it's what made him irreplaceable to Hee-yeon. Running thread distinguishing him from everyone else in the industry.

## World Building

### Korean Entertainment Industry Structure
- Hierarchy: 연습생 (trainee) → managed artists → 실장 (senior manager) → 팀장 (team lead) → 본부장 (division head/director)
- Translation: 실장 = Senior Manager; 팀장 = Team Lead; 본부장 = Director; 사장님 = Boss/CEO
- Key rhythms: fancams (직캠), monthly evals (월말평가), variety shows (예능), music shows (음방), events (행사)

### Blue Sherbet — The "Miracle Company" Legacy
- In the original timeline: company known sarcastically as "miracle company" — assembled an extraordinary lineup and still failed. Post-disbandment fancams hit 100M+ views each. All four members eventually succeeded individually.
- In the regression timeline: none of this has happened yet. Only Hyuk knows.
