## Senior Manager (실장)
- Korean term: 실장
- Category: Title / industry role
- First appearance: Chapter 1
- Definition: A senior manager or department chief level title at a Korean entertainment agency. Ranks above team lead. Hyuk Kang held this title at HS Entertainment.
- Usage notes: Rendered as "Senior Manager" in narration and formal contexts. In dialogue where someone addresses him directly, use "Manager Kang" for natural flow.
- Notes: Director Park shifts address depending on intent — flattery uses 강실장, warmth uses 강혁 씨, pressure uses 강팀장.

## Planted Article Headlines
- Category: Plot element / industry tactic
- First appearance: Chapter 12
- Definition: Three articles Director Park plants or enables, designed to pit Golden Tree's fandom against Hee-yeon and Blue Sherbet. Their purpose is to manufacture a conflict that makes Blue Sherbet look like the aggressor, damage their image, and create lasting distrust between them and established idol fandoms.
