## HS Entertainment (HS엔터테인먼트)
- Korean name: HS엔터테인먼트
- Aliases: HS Ent
- First appearance: Chapter 1
- Type: Large talent agency / entertainment company
- Description: A major agency in the Korean entertainment industry. Powerful enough that leaving on bad terms poses a genuine threat to anyone trying to launch independently.
- Significance: Hyuk Kang's former employer. The setting of the farewell dinner scene. The power structure Hyuk Kang broke free from. Confirmed in Chapter 2 as the primary cause of Blue Sherbet's failure.
- Story bible reference: Chapters 1–2
- Notes: Director Park holds a division head (본부장) position here. HS's decision to launch a boy group is what left the Blue Sherbet members waiting indefinitely at HS before Hyuk Kang recruited them.

## Hyuk Kang's Rooftop Room
- Korean name: 옥탑방
- Aliases: The rooftop
- First appearance: Chapter 1
- Type: Residence — rooftop room (옥탑방)
- Description: A small, cheap rooftop living space in a run-down neighborhood. Has an outdoor wooden platform (평상) where Hyuk Kang drinks and stargazes.
- Significance: Establishes Hyuk Kang's fallen circumstances in the future timeline. Setting of the regression trigger.
- Story bible reference: Chapter 1
- Notes: Established in Chapter 1 future-timeline framing. Does not appear in regression chapters — Hyuk Kang is not living here during the regression arc.

## LOAN's Studio Apartment — Uijeongbu (의정부 원룸)
- Korean name: 의정부 원룸
- Aliases: LOAN's place, the studio
- First appearance: Chapter 4
- Type: Residence / workspace
- Description: A small studio apartment behind a police station in Uijeongbu. Walls and windows are fully packed with soundproofing material. The room contains only a computer, composition equipment, a clothing rack, and a mattress. Used simultaneously as home and recording workspace.
- Significance: Where Hyuk and Hee-yeon meet LOAN for the first time. Setting of the breakthrough composition session.
- Story bible reference: Chapter 4
- Notes: Hyuk stopped LOAN from traveling to the company — the company has no proper workspace for composition anyway.

## KBC (KBC 방송국)
- Korean name: KBC
- Aliases: The broadcasting station, the network
- First appearance: Chapter 9
- Type: Broadcast network / television station
- Description: A major Korean broadcast network. Home of *Yeol's Music Notebook*. Hyuk and Hee-yeon visit in person to pitch the track to Producer Kim.
- Significance: The target of Hyuk's promotion campaign. Going there in person is framed as a declaration of intent — throwing down a gauntlet after a period of quiet preparation.
- Story bible reference: Chapter 9
- Notes: HS Entertainment's influence is weaker here than at music show networks, specifically because of Hae-yeol Yoo's standing. Hyuk works the floor on the way to Producer Kim — greeting every PD and writer he recognizes to maximize the word-of-mouth effect of the visit.

## The Practice Room (연습실)
- Korean name: 연습실
- Aliases: The practice room
- First appearance: Chapter 2
- Type: Training facility
- Description: Located on the outskirts. Rented from another small agency that built it in the basement of a residential building. Poor ventilation — unbearably hot in summer and freezing in winter. Where Blue Sherbet trained.
- Significance: The place where the members worked toward their debut. Emotionally significant to Hyuk — he couldn't give them better, and they gave everything they had here anyway.
- Story bible reference: Chapter 2
- Notes: Described as far inferior to HS Entertainment's facilities. A constant visual reminder of what the agency couldn't provide.

## Sinchon (신촌) — Event Venue Area
- Korean name: 신촌
- Aliases: None established
- First appearance: Chapter 12
- Type: District / outdoor event location
- Description: A district in Seoul associated with youth culture, universities, and high foot traffic. The event stage is described as 간이무대 — a makeshift, temporary outdoor stage. Open space with strong pedestrian flow and a young demographic.
- Significance: The setting of Hee-yeon's first solo event in the regression timeline. Hyuk internally notes that events require venues like this: young crowds who drive trends and open spaces that naturally pull in passersby. Hee-yeon pulls in a passing crowd organically and fully commits to converting every person present into a fan.
- Story bible reference: Chapter 12
- Notes: First on-page event location in the regression timeline. Hyuk's choice of venue reflects his deliberate thinking about event promotion as both revenue and live performance proof — fancams from events like this serve as evidence of live ability for new potential fans.
