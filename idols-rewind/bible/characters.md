## Hyuk Kang
- Korean name: 강혁
- Aliases/Titles: 강실장 (Manager Kang), 사장님 (President/Boss/CEO — used by his artists)
- First appearance: 1
- Last Updated: 2026-03-08
- Role: Protagonist and narrator. Former top-tier talent manager at HS Entertainment, now independent agency head. Regression protagonist.
- Physical description: Not described physically.
- Speech pattern: Dry, self-deprecating, controlled in social situations. Internal monologue is sharp and ruminative. Tends to reason through things aloud or on the page before arriving at conclusions. Self-corrects frequently. Uses humor as deflection.
- Dialogue cues: Speaks calmly even under pressure; delivers pointed lines with surface-level politeness ("같은 울타리 안에서 물어뜯고 싸우는 것보단 떠나는 게 낫지 않겠습니까"). In private, more blunt.
- Honorifics used toward them: 강실장 (by Park and Na at the company), 사장님 (by his artists/trainees)
- Honorifics they use toward others: 본부장님, 팀장님 (to superiors at HS); plain speech to his trainees
- Relationships: Director Park (former superior/antagonist), Team Lead Na (trusted colleague, respected), Hee-yeon Lee (former artist, now top star; unresolved emotional attachment), Jin-ah Choi / Yoon-jung Hwang / Yumi Cho (current trainees/group members)
- Story bible reference: See Novel Info — POV & Narrative Style; Voice Calibration
- Notes: Has exceptional instinct for talent, character, and commercial potential. Acknowledges this did not protect him from industry power. In the regression timeline he returns not to the beginning of his independence but to the period after Blue Sherbet's failed debut — he must rebuild from a weaker starting position than he'd prefer. Frames his past failure partly as excessive caution and misplaced confidence; intends to be more aggressive this time.

## Hee-yeon Lee
- Korean name: 이희연
- Aliases/Titles: None noted
- First appearance: 1
- Last Updated: 2026-03-08
- Role: Former member of Blue Sherbet (Kang's group); now (pre-regression present) a top star. In regression timeline, currently a trainee/early-career member under Kang.
- Physical description: Described as having glamorous appearance wholly out of place in Kang's rooftop room setting. Exact features not detailed.
- Speech pattern: Direct, persistent, emotionally honest. Refuses to back down. Speaks with quiet conviction rather than volume.
- Dialogue cues: Uses "오빠" for Kang. Pushes back on his self-deprecation with firm, specific counter-arguments.
- Honorifics used toward them: 오빠 (to Kang)
- Honorifics they use toward others: 사장님 (to Kang in regression timeline)
- Relationships: Hyuk Kang (former manager/protector; unresolved bond). Separated from other Blue Sherbet members after the group's collapse.
- Notes: In the pre-regression scene, she sought Kang out to repay a debt and offer to work together again; he refused. In the regression timeline she is a trainee who speaks to him with straightforward deference. The contrast between the two versions is narratively significant — translator should be alert to how her speech shifts between timelines.

## Jin-ah Choi
- Korean name: 최진아
- Aliases/Titles: 허세퀸 (Queen of Bluffing — Kang's internal nickname for her)
- First appearance: 2
- Last Updated: 2026-03-08
- Role: Member of Blue Sherbet. Multi-talented: vocals, dance, variety/entertainment. Post-collapse became a variety star and then a successful solo artist.
- Physical description: Described as 씹덕상 — cute, "oshi-type" face. Not conventionally the most striking visually compared to Hee-yeon and Yoon-jung but appealing in a fans-want-to-support-her way.
- Speech pattern: Loud, boastful, energetic. Habitually makes big claims about herself ("제가 또 안 되는 게 없죠"). Not always honest about mundane things (e.g. whether she bathed).
- Dialogue cues: Bursts of volume ("버럭"). Cheerful self-aggrandizement. Underneath the bluster, genuine work ethic.
- Honorifics used toward them: N/A
- Honorifics they use toward others: 언니 (to older members), plain speech to Yumi (as senior)
- Relationships: Hyuk Kang (manager/president), Blue Sherbet members
- Notes: First to appear in the regression timeline (Ch. 2). Came to fetch Kang at dawn — Kang suspects she was actually bored/lonely rather than purely diligent. Her vocal potential is not yet fully unlocked at this story point; Kang is working on it using Beyoncé's "If I Were a Boy" as a benchmark.

## Yoon-jung Hwang
- Korean name: 황윤정
- Aliases/Titles: None noted
- First appearance: 3
- Last Updated: 2026-03-08
- Role: Member of Blue Sherbet. Main dancer. Post-collapse became a major YouTuber/content creator with 10M+ subscribers; also worked as dance competition judge.
- Physical description: Tall with long limbs (길쭉길쭉한 기럭지), large eyes. Model-level visuals.
- Speech pattern: Quiet, slightly evasive. Tends to trail off or deflect when caught doing something she expects to be criticized for.
- Dialogue cues: Short, mumbling responses when embarrassed ("어···. 음···. 네···.").
- Honorifics used toward them: N/A
- Honorifics they use toward others: 언니 (to older members); 사장님 (to Kang)
- Relationships: Hyuk Kang (manager), Blue Sherbet members
- Notes: Habitually plays video games; expected criticism but Kang encourages it, foreknowing her future gaming content career. Assigned by Kang in his new plan to focus on dance, gaming/streaming content, and YouTube.

## Yumi Cho
- Korean name: 조유미
- Aliases/Titles: None noted
- First appearance: 3
- Last Updated: 2026-03-08
- Role: Youngest (막내) member of Blue Sherbet. Sub-vocalist. Post-collapse became the highest earner of the group through prolific and consistently successful songwriting.
- Physical description: 씹덕상 face, described as resembling a hamster. Cute type.
- Speech pattern: Polite, gentle, slightly self-deprecating about her work ("별로 안 좋아요").
- Dialogue cues: Warm and considerate (first thing she asks Kang is whether he's eaten). Tends to undervalue her own output.
- Honorifics used toward them: N/A
- Honorifics they use toward others: 언니 (to older members); 사장님 (to Kang)
- Relationships: Hyuk Kang (manager), Blue Sherbet members; frequent target of Jin-ah's teasing
- Notes: Has a cut finger from changing guitar strings — minor injury, not in active promotions so not consequential. Kang assigns her to focus on composition and arrangement. Her writing spans ballad, dance, rock, hip-hop, EDM.

## Director Park
- Korean name: 박본부장
- Aliases/Titles: 핏불 (Pitbull — nickname, his persistence)
- First appearance: 1
- Last Updated: 2026-03-08
- Role: Division head (본부장) at HS Entertainment. Antagonist in Ch. 1 farewell scene. Tried to retain Kang; shifted from persuasion to veiled threat when refused.
- Physical description: Not described.
- Speech pattern: Smooth, insinuating; shifts to cold and pointed when crossed. Uses proverbs as rhetorical weapons.
- Dialogue cues: Delivers threats via Korean proverbs (see Cultural Phrases). Goes cold rather than explosive when defied.
- Honorifics used toward them: 본부장님 (by Kang)
- Honorifics they use toward others: Uses 강실장, shifts to 강혁 씨 briefly, then 강팀장 as an inducement — the title shift is a deliberate tactic.
- Relationships: Hyuk Kang (former subordinate), Team Lead Na (colleague)
- Notes: Described as someone who never lets go once he bites. His warning that Kang has made himself too visible and will face industry retaliation is treated by the narrative as genuine foreshadowing, not empty bluster.

## Team Lead Na
- Korean name: 나팀장
- Aliases/Titles: 나팀장님
- First appearance: 1
- Last Updated: 2026-03-08
- Role: Team leader at HS Entertainment. Kang's closest trusted colleague and the person he most respected at the company.
- Physical description: Not described.
- Speech pattern: Not detailed; stays silent at a key moment — his silence is more significant than his words.
- Dialogue cues: Covers his face with his palm and sighs when Kang walks out — nonverbal cue.
- Honorifics used toward them: 팀장님 (by Kang)
- Notes: His choice to stay silent when Kang looks to him for support is ambiguous — Kang reads it as either withdrawal of help or unspoken wish for Kang to stay.
