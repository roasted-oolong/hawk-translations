## Hyuk Kang (강혁)
- Korean name: 강혁
- Aliases/Titles: 강실장 (Manager Kang — used by former HS colleagues and derogatorily by Team Lead Kim); 사장님 (by his artists/trainees); 강사장님 (by industry contacts); Super Manager
- First appearance: 1
- Last Updated: 2026-03-08
- Role: Protagonist and narrator. Former top-tier talent manager at HS Entertainment, now independent agency head. Regression protagonist.
- Physical description: Not described physically.
- Speech pattern: Dry, self-deprecating, controlled in social situations. Internal monologue is sharp and ruminative. Tends to reason through things aloud or on the page before arriving at conclusions. Self-corrects frequently. Uses humor as deflection.
- Dialogue cues: Speaks calmly even under pressure; delivers pointed lines with surface-level politeness ("같은 울타리 안에서 물어뜯고 싸우는 것보단 떠나는 게 낫지 않겠습니까"). In private, more blunt. Ch. 30 — publicly refuses "강실장" title, demands "강사장님" as a deliberate industry-signal move.
- Honorifics used toward them: 강실장 (former HS colleagues; derogatorily by Team Lead Kim); 사장님 (by his artists/trainees); 강사장님 (by industry contacts)
- Honorifics they use toward others: 본부장님, 팀장님 (to HS superiors); plain speech to his trainees
- Relationships: Director Park (former superior/antagonist), Team Lead Na (trusted colleague), Hee-yeon Lee (former artist, now top star; unresolved emotional attachment), Jin-ah Choi / Yoon-jung Hwang / Yumi Cho (current trainees/group members), Jung-han Yoo (sole employee; carries guilt toward him), Sung-ah Kim (former artist; close enough for 오빠)
- Story bible reference: See Novel Info — POV & Narrative Style; Voice Calibration
- Notes: Has exceptional instinct for talent, character, and commercial potential. Acknowledges this did not protect him from industry power. In the regression timeline he returns to the period after Blue Sherbet's failed debut — must rebuild from a weaker starting position. Frames past failure partly as excessive caution and misplaced confidence; intends to be more aggressive this time. Ch. 28 — learns from Hye-jung Yoon that multiple industry figures are actively watching K Management and considering joining when contracts expire. Ch. 30 — direct confrontation with Team Lead Kim in salon; adopts deliberately blunt, aggressive register to send a public industry signal. Ch. 31 — visible pre-stage anxiety despite experience; watches Jin-ah's debut with pride.

- Korean name: 최진아

- First appearance: 2
- Last Updated: 2026-03-08

- Physical description: Described as 씹덕상 — cute, "oshi-type" face. Not conventionally the most striking visually compared to Hee-yeon and Yoon-jung but appealing in a fans-want-to-support-her way.
- Speech pattern: Loud, boastful, energetic. Habitually makes big claims about herself ("제가 또 안 되는 게 없죠"). Not always honest about mundane things (e.g. whether she bathed).
- Dialogue cues: Bursts of volume ("버럭"). Cheerful self-aggrandizement. Underneath the bluster, genuine work ethic.
- Honorifics used toward them: N/A
- Honorifics they use toward others: 언니 (to older members), plain speech to Yumi (as senior)
- Relationships: Hyuk Kang (manager/president), Blue Sherbet members
- Story bible reference: —
- Notes: First to appear in the regression timeline (Ch. 2). Came to fetch Kang at dawn — Kang suspects she was actually bored/lonely rather than purely diligent. Her vocal potential is not yet fully unlocked at this story point; Kang is working on it using Beyoncé's "If I Were a Boy" as a benchmark. Ch. 21 — Yoo-mi selects her as vocalist for the EDM track, citing her husky voice and versatility. Her contribution: a single spoken/narrated line ("I don't love anything"), delivered in one take with no warm-up. Her initial dismissal of the task ("보컬이 아니잖아!") turns to stunned silence once she hears how her voice is processed in the finished mix. Promises Yoo-mi a jelly purchase she then refuses to acknowledge.
