## Hyuk Kang (강혁)
- Korean name: 강혁
- Role: Protagonist. Former Senior Manager at HS Entertainment. Founder and CEO of K Management.
- First appearance: Chapter 1
- Background: Top-tier talent manager in his original timeline. Left HS Entertainment to launch Blue Sherbet independently — failed due to HS interference and his own passivity. Woke up after the regression at the point just after Blue Sherbet's first failed debut.
- Relationships: Former employer Director Park (antagonist). All four Blue Sherbet members (artists / surrogate family dynamic). LOAN, Hae-yeol Yoo, Jong-woo Kim (industry allies). Na Team Lead (former superior, now joining K Management as of Ch. 25).
- Address: Members call him 사장님 (Boss) or, in warmer/emotional moments, 오빠 (Jin-ah most commonly). Industry contacts use 강사장님. Na Team Lead uses 인마 / 야 (affectionate-gruff senior address).
- Speech pattern: Dry, precise, rarely wastes words. Internal monologue is the richest register — rhetorical questions, self-corrections, reasoning chains built out loud. In dialogue he is brief, often deflecting compliments or affection with a slight edge. Warmth comes through indirectly. Never explains himself unless necessary.
- Notes: Does not show anxiety externally. When he worries, it reads as calm assessment. The reader sees his uncertainty through narration, not behavior.

## Hee-yeon Lee (이희연)
- Korean name: 이희연
- Role: Main vocalist of Blue Sherbet. First member to launch solo (regression timeline).
- First appearance: Chapter 1 (future timeline); Chapter 2 (regression timeline)
- Background: Became a solo success in the original timeline — hollow without her group and Hyuk. Tracked Hyuk down in the future to bring him back. Her conviction that he was never wrong is core to his motivation.
- Solo debut: "Wish Upon A Moon" (달에게 소원을). Peaked #12. Still charting weeks later as of Ch. 13.
- Current arc (Chs. 13–25): Rock track in development with Hae-yeol Yoo and LOAN. Demo recording is already excellent by Hae-yeol and LOAN's assessment — she refuses to release it, says she can do better. Hyuk backs her instinct. Outcome unresolved as of Ch. 25.
- Relationships: Hyuk (mentor / the person she believes in most). Jin-ah (comfortable older sister dynamic). LOAN and Hae-yeol (working relationship, current tension around perfectionism).
- Address: Calls Hyuk 사장님. Referred to by Hyuk as 희연이.
- Speech pattern: Calm, measured, direct. Doesn't soften positions when challenged. States her case plainly and holds it without defensiveness. Warmth shows in small gestures rather than words.
- Notes: Has not shown perfectionist tendencies before Ch. 21. Her hold on the rock recording is the first time she has pushed back against professional consensus. Hyuk reads this as a genuine signal, not a block.

## Jin-ah Choi (최진아)
- Korean name: 최진아
- Role: Member of Blue Sherbet. Lead performer / dance vocalist. Solo debut confirmed Ch. 23.
- First appearance: Chapter 2
- Background: Dance singer in the original timeline — became one of the best in the country. Regression timeline: solo debut confirmed after evaluation in Ch. 23.
- Solo concept: 여제 (Empress / Female Emperor). Fantasy setting. Palace, attendants (=dancers), no subjects. Hard contrast to her off-stage personality. MV budget: 100 million won. Choreography: co-created by Yoon-jung and Aligator's Toe Dance (Ah-young). Song composed by LOAN. MV director: Jong-woo Kim.
- Vocal training: Now in sessions. Trainer is enthusiastic — active fine-tuning, not remediation.
- Current arc (Chs. 13–25): Records vocal for Yumi's track (single line, heavily processed — one take). Solo debut confirmed. In vocal training and concept prep.
- Relationships: Hyuk (calls him 오빠 in emotional moments, 사장님 otherwise — slides between the two). Yumi (bickering sisterly dynamic). Hee-yeon (comfortable older sister relationship). Yoon-jung (warmth).
- Address: Calls Hyuk 사장님 / 오빠. Hyuk calls her 진아 / 진아야.
- Speech pattern: Rapid-fire emotional outbursts, then embarrassed backpedaling. Physical — hugs, cries, reaches for people. Mixes jondaemal and banmal with Hyuk without noticing. Loud. Does impression bits (cute, not funny by Hyuk's assessment). Competitive about being acknowledged.
- Notes: The gap between her stage presence (overwhelming, dominant) and her everyday personality (loud, clumsy, affectionate) is a deliberate character contrast the novel treats as a major hook. She has no idea how good she is.

## Yoon-jung Hwang (황윤정)
- Korean name: 황윤정
- Role: Main dancer of Blue Sherbet. Internet streamer, YouTuber, dancer.
- First appearance: Chapter 2
- Background: Became Korea's top dance YouTuber in the original timeline — 1 million subscribers as a solo act. Regression timeline: streaming and YouTube growing rapidly.
- Current stats (as of Ch. 14): Average 1,200 live viewers; ~80,000 YouTube subscribers; daily stream income ~600,000 won (exceeds K Management's revenue).
- Current arc (Chs. 13–25): First dance video uploaded (Ch. 15) — immediate breakout. Aligator's Toe Dance comparison goes viral (Ch. 16). Battle shoot with Kim Ah-young: Yoon-jung chooses crump, Ah-young chooses poppin/dubstep. Yoon-jung wins the crowd. All external interview/photo requests declined by Hyuk — she's in a growth phase. Placed at Aligator's Toe Dance to co-create Jin-ah's choreography (Ch. 24).
- Relationships: Hyuk (complete trust). Kim Ah-young (new friendship forming). Yumi (warm). Jin-ah (warm, teasing).
- Address: Calls Hyuk 사장님. Hyuk calls her 윤정이 / 윤정아.
- Speech pattern: Highest energy of all members. Pure reactions, no filter. Swings rapidly between panic and all-in. Squeals. Rhetorical ("어떻게 그렇게 태연하세요?" type). Infectious enthusiasm is the core of her on-stage appeal.
- Notes: Hyuk's read — her ability to transmit joy is her true talent, beyond technical skill. The audience doesn't just watch her; they feel what she feels.

## Yumi Cho (조유미)
- Korean name: 조유미
- Role: Sub-vocalist / youngest member (maknae) of Blue Sherbet. Emerging composer-producer.
- First appearance: Chapter 2
- Background: In the original timeline, became a composer much later. Regression timeline: accelerating far ahead of schedule.
- Current arc (Chs. 13–25): Composes an EDM track as a side experiment (Ch. 13). Hyuk decides not to release it as a single — uploads to YouTube/SoundCloud to build experience. Uploads 25 tracks total (24 Daft Punk remixes + 1 original) at roughly 1 track/hour. Had no idea this rate was unusual. DJ Sharkin (overseas EDM artist) discovers her work and shouts her out on SNS live (Ch. 20) — catalyst for international traction. Records vocal for her own track: one line ("I don't love anything"), heavily processed (Ch. 21). Single released with cyberpunk animated MV (Ch. 22). Overseas: enthusiastic niche reception. Domestic: indifferent/negative as expected.
- Relationships: Jin-ah (bickering sisterly dynamic — genuine affection underneath). Hyuk (deference, clean trust). Hae-yeol's studio engineer (working relationship, Ch. 21).
- Address: Calls Hyuk 사장님. Hyuk calls her 유미 / 유미야.
- Speech pattern: Gentle, earnest, slightly mischievous with Jin-ah. Zero ego about her work — "그냥요?" is her default explanation for extraordinary output. Delegates all external decisions to Hyuk without hesitation. Quiet. Doesn't seek validation.
- Notes: Was always planning to move on to other genres after Daft Punk — EDM wasn't a fixation, it was just where she started. She didn't realize 24 remixes in a few days was unusual.

## LOAN (로안)
- Korean name: 로안
- Role: Composer-producer. Freelance. K Management's de facto first-call composer.
- First appearance: Chapter 3 (mentioned); Chapter 4 (full introduction)
- Background: Future head producer at HS Entertainment in original timeline. Regression timeline: intercepted before HS gets him.
- Current arc (Chs. 13–25): Meets Hae-yeol Yoo; they work jointly on Hee-yeon's rock track (painful process — opposite styles, multiple rewrites). Registers a legal entity with help from Hyuk and Hae-yeol (Ch. 23 — small studio + office). Declines all HS Entertainment offers. Prioritizes K Management above all other work.
- Relationships: Hyuk (deep loyalty, feels a growing debt he can't repay). Hae-yeol (productive friction — opposite styles, mutual respect). Blue Sherbet members (warm, professional).
- Address: Calls Hyuk 사장님. Hyuk calls him 로안 작곡가님.
- Speech pattern: Deferential, self-deprecating about his own standing — but the deference is now chosen rather than nervous. He knows his value; he just genuinely means it. Becomes flustered when praised directly.
- Notes: Always "LOAN" (all caps). Confirmed male (Ch. 4). Told Hyuk directly in the car (Ch. 14) that he wants to join K Management — Hyuk declined for now (can't provide proper studio yet) but promised to help him incorporate independently. That promise is now fulfilled.

## Hae-yeol Yoo (유해열)
- Korean name: 유해열
- Role: Established singer-songwriter. Industry veteran. Informal ally of K Management.
- First appearance: Chapter 9 (mentioned); Chapter 10 (full introduction)
- Background: Beloved industry figure. Hosts *Yeol's Music Notebook*. Beyond HS's reach. Took an interest in Hee-yeon after the broadcast.
- Current arc (Chs. 13–25): Calls Hyuk to request a LOAN connection — can't find the right sound for Hee-yeon's next track alone (Ch. 14). Works jointly with LOAN on the rock track — exhausting but productive. Believes the demo is already release-worthy. Frustrated that Hyuk sides with Hee-yeon over his assessment (Ch. 21). Helped LOAN set up his legal entity.
- Relationships: Hyuk (mutual respect, increasing warmth). LOAN (opposite styles, productive friction). Hee-yeon (genuine admiration — described her as "someone who reignites" him).
- Address: Calls Hyuk 강사장님. Hyuk calls him 유해열 사장님.
- Speech pattern: Warm, slightly self-deprecating about age, dry humor. Expressive about exhaustion. Lets people know when he's working hard.

## Director Park (박본부장 / 박도진 — NOTE: separate character below)
- Korean name: 박본부장
- Role: Division Head at HS Entertainment. Primary antagonist.
- First appearance: Chapter 1
- Background: Blocked Blue Sherbet in the original timeline. In the regression timeline, has been systematically attempting to destroy K Management's momentum.
- Current arc (Chs. 13–25): Dating scandal attempt (Ch. 18–19) backfired catastrophically — Yoon-jung was live on stream, Hee-yeon's whereabouts were publicly documented. Hired operative Park Do-jin and journalist Choi Ho-woo both go dark after the backfire. Director Park begins to fear Hyuk knew in advance and staged the counter. Daily intimidation of Team 3 (especially Na Team Lead) intensifying. Psychological state shifting from calculated aggression to paranoia.
- Address: Calls Hyuk 강실장 (flattery), 강혁 씨 (warmth), 강팀장 (pressure). HS staff call him 본부장님.
- Speech pattern: Controlled, rhetorical, uses proverbs as pressure tools. Currently: tightly wound, prone to eruptions, conducting daily blame sessions against Team 3.
- Notes: His paranoia that Hyuk may have known about the scandal in advance is new — this is a psychological shift worth tracking.

## Na Team Lead (나팀장)
- Korean name: 나팀장
- Role: Team Lead, HS Entertainment Team 3. Hyuk's former direct superior. Joining K Management as of Ch. 25.
- First appearance: Chapter 1 (background / stayed silent during Director Park's threat); Chapter 13 (observed from afar); Chapter 25 (on-page, recruited)
- Background: Hyuk's former direct superior at HS. Hyuk describes him as the rarest kind of boss — fair, principled, never abused rank, never punished anyone for doing their job well. Cannot advance further at HS because integrity is a liability in the politics there. His team (Team 3) is the only unit at HS that openly roots for Hyuk.
- Significance: First major hire at K Management. Fills the right-hand gap that Yoo Jung-han (loyal but still learning) cannot yet fill. Intends to eventually bring Jang Senior Manager and Shin Senior Manager over.
- Address: Hyuk calls him 나팀장 / 팀장님. Na Team Lead calls Hyuk 인마 (affectionate rough address for a junior male), occasionally 야. Warm but gruff.
- Speech pattern: Direct, no-nonsense. Warm underneath but doesn't show it easily. Says exactly what he means. Calls Director Park "그 새끼" in private. When he wants to say something, he says it — but won't perform loyalty for show.
- Notes: His agreement to join in Ch. 25 came without long deliberation — the fact that he came to the meeting at all signaled his answer. He asked about Jang and Shin before confirming, which is characteristic.

## Yoo Jung-han (유정한)
- Korean name: 유정한
- Role: Manager at K Management. First employee. Handles logistics, pickups, scheduling.
- First appearance: Chapter 2
- Background: The founding loyalist at K Management. Still learning — Hyuk notes in Ch. 25 that while he is the true 개국공신 (founding contributor), he is not yet capable of being Hyuk's right hand.

- Address: Calls Hyuk 형님. Hyuk calls him 정한.
- Speech pattern: Earnest, occasionally over-eager. Growing into his role. Good instincts that occasionally outrun his experience.

- Korean name: 김아영
- Role: Head choreographer / de facto leader of Aligator's Toe Dance (앨토).
- First appearance: Chapter 16
- Background: Known choreographer. Has worked with idol groups. Internationally recognized (500K YouTube subscribers, mostly overseas). National recognition limited. Competitive toward 777 Dance Studio — considers them a target, not an enemy.

## Jong-woo Kim (김종우)
- Korean name: 김종우
- Role: MV director. Film Studies student at Daehan Arts University (still enrolled).
- First appearance: Chapter 7
- Background: Recruited by Hyuk at his university. Co-directed "Wish Upon A Moon" MV. Described as someone whose passion and instinct produce efficiency far beyond his budget level.

Has a visible crush on Yoon-jung; Hyuk notices.
- Address: Calls Hyuk 사장님. Hyuk calls him 감독님.
- Speech pattern: Rapid, enthusiastic, talks at length about his craft when given the chance. Self-aware about being a student but not self-conscious about his passion. Genuine warmth.
