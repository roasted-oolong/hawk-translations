## Jung-han's Idiom Escalation Stack — 좌청룡 우백호 / 좌방통 우제갈량
- Korean phrases: 좌청룡 우백호 (Left Azure Dragon, Right White Tiger) / 좌방통 우제갈량 (Left Pang Tong, Right Zhuge Liang)
- Literal translation: Azure Dragon on the left, White Tiger on the right / Pang Tong on the left, Zhuge Liang on the right
- Intended meaning: 좌청룡 우백호 — classical Chinese/Korean cosmological symbols for perfect balance and ideal conditions; idiomatically means "the complete package" or "couldn't be better." 좌방통 우제갈량 — two legendary strategists from Romance of the Three Kingdoms, paired as an invincible duo; implies a combination so powerful it's historically unprecedented.
- Context: Jung-han's running comic habit — each round of praise escalates one tier higher. Round 1 (Ch. 5): pretty + good at games = 금상첨화 (cherry on top). Round 2: fun + skill = 좌청룡 우백호. Round 3: active idol + dancer = 좌방통 우제갈량.
- Established translation: Preserve the escalation as a three-tier stack with each tier punching higher. Render naturally in English — no need to translate the classical references literally. Suggested: Round 1: "cherry on top" / Round 2: "the whole package" / Round 3: "a once-in-a-generation combination" or equivalent that signals the joke has gone somewhere absurd.
- T/N written: No — localized via escalation structure
- First appearance: Chapter 5
- Notes: The comedy comes from Jung-han's instinct to always reach for one more idiom. Preserve the rhythm — three beats, each more elaborate.
