## A tree that will flourish is known from its first leaves
- Korean phrase: 될성부른 나무는 떡잎부터 알아본다
- Literal translation: A tree that will grow well is recognized from its cotyledons
- Intended meaning: Talent and promise are visible from the very beginning
- Context: Park Bu-jang uses this to compliment Kang — implying he spotted Kang's talent early — as part of a persuasion/retention attempt.

- Notes: Delivered as a sequence with the next two proverbs; the trio builds from flattery → warning → threat. Translation should preserve the rhetorical escalation.

## The awl in the sack
- Korean phrase: 낭중지추 (囊中之錐)
- Literal translation: The awl/point inside the bag — a sharp object that will poke through no matter what
- Intended meaning: Outstanding talent cannot be hidden; it will make itself known
- Context: Second in Park Bu-jang's trio of proverbs; still flattery, but edging toward warning — Kang is too visible.

## A protruding stone gets struck by the chisel
- Korean phrase: 모난 돌은 정 맞는다
- Literal translation: A stone with corners gets hit by the stonemason's chisel
- Intended meaning: Someone who stands out or is difficult will be cut down; don't make yourself a target
- Context: Third in Park Bu-jang's trio; this one is the open threat. Kang falls silent; Na Team Leader protests the use of it.
- Established translation:
- T/N written: no
- T/N text:
- First appearance: 1
- Last Updated: 2026-03-08
- Notes: The most pointed of the three — Park's rhetorical move is to dress a threat in proverbial form and then deny it's a threat. Tone in English must carry that deniability.

## 씹덕상
- Korean phrase: 씹덕상
- Literal translation: "Ultimate otaku face/type" — portmanteau of 씹- (intensifier) + 덕후 (otaku/fan-type) + 상 (face/looks)
- Intended meaning: A face/look that is intensely appealing to fans/hobbyists in a cute, adorable way — the kind of face that inspires devoted fandom
- Context: Kang uses it internally to describe both Jin-ah and Yu-mi. Affectionate, not derogatory in his internal register.
- Established translation:
- T/N written: no
- T/N text:
- First appearance: 2
- Last Updated: 2026-03-08
- Notes: Colloquial and internet-native term. Likely needs a loose English equivalent rather than a literal render. "Fan-favorite face," "irresistibly stans-able look," or similar depending on target register (YA audience).
