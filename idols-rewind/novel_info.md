## Title
- Korean: 다시 키우는 걸그룹
- English (working title): Idols Rewind

## Summary
A former top-tier talent manager who lost everything gets a second chance when he wakes up back in the past. Hyuk Kang had the sharpest eye in the industry — he could read talent, character, and potential better than anyone — but integrity alone couldn't protect him from the people with real power. His independent agency failed, his artists scattered, and years later he's living in a rooftop room with nothing to show for it. Then the full moon shimmers strangely overhead, and he wakes up with everything to do over. This time, he knows exactly what mistakes not to make.

## Genre
Idol industry / regression / music & performance / slice of industry life

## Target Audience
Young Adult

## Tone
Dry, introspective, and bittersweet. The internal monologue is sharp and self-deprecating with quiet humor. Confident pacing — emotional without being melodramatic. The idol industry backdrop gives it energy and ambition, but the emotional core is about regret, integrity, and second chances.

## Setting
- Time period: Modern — present day and regression to the past (timeline TBD)
- World type: Real-world modern Korea, grounded. No fantasy elements beyond the regression mechanic.

## POV & Narrative Style
First-person past tense. Tight internal monologue — the reader lives inside Hyuk Kang's head. Scenes are structured with a clear sense of before and after; Chapter 1 uses a two-scene structure (past farewell → present rock bottom) to establish the full arc of his fall before the regression begins.

Kang frequently thinks through ideas on the page rather than simply delivering conclusions — rhetorical questions, self-corrections (*"Maybe. No. Definitely."*), reasoning chains that build out loud before arriving somewhere. This is core to his voice. During localization, resist the pull to collapse these into clean declarative statements. The reader should watch him think, not just receive his conclusions. Crispness that removes the thinking removes the character.
